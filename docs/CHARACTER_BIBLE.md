# Character Bible

## Singer

### Description

A charismatic adult country singer with expressive comic timing. Cheerful, confident, playful, and knowingly campy without becoming explicit.

### Wardrobe Direction

- Voluminous country hairstyle
- Gingham blouse or country dress
- Denim accents
- Worn but attractive boots
- Warm, playful facial expressions

## Cowboy

### Description

A ruggedly handsome adult country cook who plays the premise completely straight, as if starring in the world’s most wholesome breakfast commercial.

### Wardrobe Direction

- Weathered cowboy hat
- Denim jeans
- Leather boots
- Farmhouse cooking apron
- Rolled sleeves or tasteful workwear
- Friendly, slightly exaggerated commercial smile

## Irish Setter (Recurring Character / Prop)

### Description

A handsome adult Irish Setter with a glossy red-gold coat and long silky ears. He is a warm, wholesome farmhouse presence who gets underfoot at exactly the wrong moments. He is part of the breakfast-commercial charm, not a cartoon mascot.

### Performance Direction

- Adult dog energy only; never childlike, puppy-like, or cartoon mascot.
- Uses him for physical comedy: underfoot during mixing, hopeful at the table, curious about syrup/jam that gets rejected.
- Same dog appearance, coloring, and size across every shot.
- Never steals the lyric-focus for more than a cut; he supports the food-commercial gags.

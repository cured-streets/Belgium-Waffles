# Visual Style Guide

## Locked Direction

**Adult retro country breakfast commercial parody.**

The video should feel like adult performers in a wholesome vintage waffle commercial that accidentally became a knowingly ridiculous country music video. The humor comes from deadpan sincerity, food-commercial staging, lyric timing, and exaggerated reactions — not explicit visuals.

## Core Visual Formula

```text
1970s country variety show
+ vintage farmhouse breakfast commercial
+ soft-focus adult romance-commercial parody
+ warm food photography
+ campy deadpan performance
```

## Primary Look

- Warm 1970s / Kodachrome-inspired color
- Golden farmhouse morning light
- Rustic wood, gingham red, faded denim blue, cream ceramic props
- Slightly faded blacks
- Soft highlight bloom
- Tasteful film grain
- Food-commercial steam, butter, syrup, flour, and waffle hero shots
- Adult country-western wardrobe and styling
- Performers clearly adult in every shot

## What We Are Avoiding

Because the song is adult innuendo, the video should avoid kid-coded or family-cartoon associations.

Avoid:

- Child characters
- Saturday-morning cartoon style
- Family sitcom cartoon framing as the main look
- Direct Flintstones / Jetsons / Hanna-Barbera imitation
- Recognizable cartoon character silhouettes, costumes, names, or settings
- Toy-like, nursery-like, or children’s-book styling
- Any visual that makes the joke feel aimed at or adjacent to children

## How to Use the Uploaded Cartoon References

The uploaded cartoon references are useful only as broad caution/inspiration references for:

- Comedic breakfast staging
- Absurdly sincere food presentation
- Retro TV composition
- Exaggerated product-commercial smiles
- Waffle gag clarity

They should **not** be used as direct style targets.

## Safe Prompt Language

Use phrases like:

```text
adult retro country breakfast commercial, 1970s farmhouse kitchen, vintage food advertising photography, campy adult performers, wholesome on-screen cooking, playful innuendo through reactions only, warm Kodachrome color, soft film grain, golden morning light, rustic wood and gingham, country variety show energy
```

## Unsafe / Avoid Prompt Language

Avoid phrases like:

```text
children's cartoon, Saturday morning cartoon, Flintstones, Jetsons, Hanna-Barbera, family cartoon, kid-friendly, toy-like, childlike, prehistoric cartoon family, cartoon sitcom characters
```

## Performance Rule

The performers should behave as if this is the most wholesome breakfast commercial ever filmed. They should never make the innuendo visually explicit.

## Packaging Rule

All packaging text must be created manually in post-production. Generated boxes should have blank label panels only.

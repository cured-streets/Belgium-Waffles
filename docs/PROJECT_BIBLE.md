## Core Concept

A cheerful, campy country music video that plays a suggestive song completely straight as a wholesome farmhouse waffle commercial. The visuals stay literally focused on cooking, batter, breakfast props, and exaggerated food-commercial performance.

## Creative Guardrails

1. All human characters must clearly be adults.
2. Keep visuals literally focused on cooking and waffles.
3. No nudity, explicit sexual behavior, or anatomical imagery.
4. Humor comes from lyrics, reactions, editing, and food-commercial framing.
5. No direct imitation of living celebrities.
6. All readable text is created manually in post-production.
7. Avoid recognizable commercial trademarks unless cleared.

## Visual Direction

Warm 1970s country variety show meets vintage waffle commercial: golden morning light, gingham red, faded denim, rustic wood, flour in sunbeams, steam, cast iron, and soft film grain.

The selected direction is **adult retro country breakfast commercial parody**, not kid-coded cartoon parody. The uploaded cartoon references may inform broad comedic staging, but the final video should avoid Saturday-morning cartoon, family-cartoon, childlike, or recognizable franchise associations.

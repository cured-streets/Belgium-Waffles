# Baby Batter for Breakfast — AI Music Video

A two-minute, tongue-in-cheek country music video built with a hybrid workflow:

- AI-generated cinematic still images
- Selected image-to-video hero shots
- Editorial motion applied to stills
- Practical overlays such as steam, flour, film grain, and light leaks
- Titles, labels, and other readable text created manually in post-production

The visual joke should remain playful and implied. On screen, everything is literally about making Belgian waffles in a farmhouse kitchen.

## Creative Brief

### Concept

A charismatic country singer wakes in a sunny farmhouse and discovers her handsome cowboy making his special waffle batter.

The production plays the lyrics completely straight. The performers act cheerful, wholesome, and deadpan while food-commercial photography and suggestive visual timing carry the double entendre.

The video builds from a peaceful farmhouse morning into a flour-covered country-kitchen celebration.

### Tone

- Campy
- Flirtatious
- Cheerful
- Wholesome on the surface

- Retro country-commercial parody
- Suggestive through implication, never explicit

## Creative Guardrails

1. All human characters must clearly be adults.
2. Keep the visuals literally focused on cooking and waffles.
3. Do not include nudity, explicit sexual behavior, or anatomical imagery.
4. The humor comes from lyrics, reactions, editing, and food-commercial framing.
5. Performances should feel knowingly campy without becoming graphic.
6. Avoid direct imitation of a living celebrity.
7. Do not rely on AI generators for readable lettering.
8. Product labels, opening titles, credits, and captions must be added in post.
9. Avoid recognizable commercial trademarks unless their use has been cleared.
10. Do not use an artist’s face or another person’s likeness without permission.

## Production Strategy

This project follows the hybrid method previously used for the music video *Take the Streets Back*:

1. Generate and approve recurring characters and locations.
2. Create cinematic key-frame stills for the complete timeline.
3. Select only the strongest shots for image-to-video generation.
4. Fill the remaining timeline with animated stills.
5. Add slow push-ins, pans, parallax, overlays, and transitions in the editor.
6. Create all readable text manually.
7. Finish with color matching, grain, sound accents, and final export.

This method improves consistency and avoids generating two uninterrupted minutes of unstable AI video.

### Approximate Content Balance

- **35–50 seconds:** Generated video
- **60–80 seconds:** Animated still images
- **10–20 seconds:** Product shots, inserts, transitions, and editor-created graphics

These numbers may change after the final audio is timed.

## Preliminary Two-Minute Story Structure

Exact timing will be adjusted after the final audio master is added.

### Intro and Verse 1

- Singer wakes in the farmhouse
- She smells breakfast and walks toward the kitchen
- Cowboy stands heroically at the stove
- Inserts of boots, hat, stove, and cooking tools
- Bacon and ham are presented, then dismissed
- Cowboy reveals the ceramic mixing bowl

### Chorus 1

- Slow-motion batter pour
- Batter lands on the waffle iron
- Steam rises dramatically
- Singer waits with fork and knife
- Singer performs into a whisk
- First hero shot of the finished waffle

### Verse 2

- Maple syrup and jam are pushed aside
- Cowboy mixes his special recipe
- Singer watches with exaggerated admiration
- Batter or flour lands harmlessly on her nose
- Reaction shot and playful wink

### Chorus 2

- Midday waffle lunch
- Candlelit waffle supper
- Romantic clinking of milk glasses
- Increasing kitchen chaos
- Waffle stacks and food-commercial inserts

### Bridge

- Moody soft-focus lighting
- Close-ups of hands whisking batter
- Cowboy concentrates dramatically
- Singer fans herself with a recipe card
- Couple stirs the bowl together
- Gentle camera push-in and romantic lens flare

### Final Chorus and Outro

- High-energy kitchen performance
- Waffle flip
- Flour burst
- Couple dances around the kitchen island
- Finished waffle hero shot
- Cowboy places a blank retro box on the counter
- Product label is added in post
- Cowboy gives the camera a thumbs-up
- Final freeze frame or short product hold

## Image-to-Video Guidelines

1. Begin with an approved still whenever possible.
2. Ask for one primary action per generation.
3. Use simple, physically plausible camera movement.
4. Keep facial motion subtle.
5. Avoid complicated hand choreography.
6. Generate short clips and cut around unstable frames.
7. Preserve space for cropping and stabilization.
8. Do not ask the model to create readable package text.
9. Keep motion intensity moderate unless the shot is intentionally chaotic.
10. Save the source image, motion prompt, model version, and output together.

Example:

> Slow camera push toward the adult cowboy standing at the stove. He turns his head toward the camera and gives a restrained smile. Steam rises gently behind him. Warm morning sunlight and floating flour remain consistent. Natural movement, stable face, stable hands, cinematic 24 fps.

## Editorial Plan

### Still-Image Motion

Use subtle motion to keep stills from feeling static:

- Scale from 100% to approximately 106%
- Pan across strong horizontal compositions
- Separate foreground and background for parallax
- Add restrained handheld drift
- Animate steam and flour independently
- Avoid excessive movement on every shot

### Rhythm

- Verses: slower narrative coverage
- Choruses: faster food inserts and performance shots
- Bridge: romantic slow motion and close-ups
- Final chorus: fastest cutting and largest motion
- Outro: clear product hold and visual punchline

### Transitions

Preferred:

- Straight cuts
- Whip pans used sparingly
- Steam or flour wipes
- Match cuts between bowls, waffles, and circular objects
- Light leaks at structural song transitions

Avoid excessive template transitions.

### Color Finish

All sources should be matched to:

- Warm highlights
- Creamy skin tones
- Slightly faded blacks
- Restrained film grain
- Soft highlight bloom
- Consistent gingham red and denim blue
- Natural food color
